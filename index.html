About HisabKitab

Less paperwork. More clarity.

Running a shop means balancing customers, stock, sales and payments at the same time. HisabKitab is designed to bring these everyday tasks into one organised workflow, with simple screens and information that is easy to find.

Whether you keep customer credit, check product availability or follow up on a pending payment, the goal is the same: make day-to-day business easier to manage.

Frequently asked questions

Some quick information about HisabKitab.

What is HisabKitab?

HisabKitab is a shop-management app concept focused on customer khata, sales, payments, inventory and customer follow-ups.

Can I record partial payments?

The intended workflow keeps sales and received payments as separate transactions, helping the remaining balance stay clear.

Can I track product stock?

The inventory workflow is designed for product quantities, units and prices. Availability depends on the features included in the current app release.

Is the app available to download?

Official download links can be added here once the app is published. Contact us for launch information.
