Our purpose

Built around everyday shopkeeping

HisabKitab aims to make routine business records easier to manage by bringing customer khata, sales, payments, inventory and follow-ups into one place.

Why HisabKitab?

Shop owners make dozens of small decisions every day: what was sold, which payment arrived, what a customer still owes, and which product needs restocking. When these details are spread across notebooks, messages and separate tools, it becomes harder to see the full picture.

HisabKitab is being shaped around these everyday needs, with a focus on clarity, simple workflows and records that are easy to review.

Our approach

Keep it practical. The goal is to make common shop tasks straightforward, not add unnecessary complexity.

Keep records clear. Sales, payments and returns should be understandable as separate transactions.

Support better decisions. Useful summaries can help shopkeepers see pending dues, stock levels and customer activity.

Who is it for?

HisabKitab is intended for small and growing retailers, including grocery stores, clothing shops, mobile accessory stores and neighbourhood businesses. Feature availability may vary by release.
